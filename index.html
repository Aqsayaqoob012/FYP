Process

How It Works

Four simple steps from environment setup to actionable insights delivered in real time.

01
Environment Setup & Background Calibration

Clear the frame, configure camera angles, and let Monitor AI learn the static background. This baseline enables precise change detection and dramatically reduces false positives.

02
Optimal Lighting Conditions

Ensure adequate illumination for both visible-spectrum and infrared cameras. Monitor AI auto-adjusts exposure parameters and leverages low-light enhancement algorithms for 24/7 reliability.

03
Accurate AI Detection Process

Our multi-stage neural pipeline runs object classification, person tracking, audio analysis, and behavioral scoring simultaneously — all on a single inference pass per frame.

04
Real-Time Monitoring & Reporting

Alerts surface instantly in the dashboard. Evidence is auto-captured, scored, and attached to structured reports that are sent to your team via email, webhook, or API callback.
